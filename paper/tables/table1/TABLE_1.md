# Table 1 | Four research strands and their connections to the evaluation questions.

| Research strand | Representative work | Existing capabilities | Measurement or implementation here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Factual-information conditioning | FOHL, OOSR, Zhang et al. | Counterfactual prediction from factual observations or treatment-selection regret evaluation | State-conditioned correct-versus-shuffled suffix value in reactive traffic |
| Interaction and multi-action prediction | WIMP, ProSim, Fujii et al., CF-GODE | Controllable prediction, reactive rollout or multi-agent intervention modelling | Two-layer evaluation of input value and action consequences for fixed event records |
| Decision-relevant quality and selective prediction | Farid, Nakamura, CCTR, ConfidNet, SelectiveNet | Cost-related failure evaluation, calibration or learned retention ranking | Direct supervision by candidate-action regret after freezing the selector |
| Rejection, risk control and expert deferral | RwR, conformal risk control, learning-to-defer | Rejection, risk guarantees or model–expert collaboration under specified losses/assumptions | Empirical offline retention of decision groups |
