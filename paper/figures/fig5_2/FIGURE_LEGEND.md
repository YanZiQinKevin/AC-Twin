# Direct audit head architecture

**Direct audit head architecture and joint supervision.** **a,** Nine competing actions are represented by relative-comparison tokens, each comprising 20 continuous features and 14 one-hot indicators. The flattened 306-dimensional representation is concatenated with nine global features. Normalization statistics are fitted on the designated training data; one-hot indicators remain unchanged. **b,** The fixed 315–43–43–2 MLP has 15,740 trainable parameters. Both hidden layers use Linear–LayerNorm–ReLU–Dropout with a dropout rate of 0.10. The two outputs are raw_mu and logit_high; softplus transforms raw_mu into the non-negative continuous output. **c,** True decision regret provides continuous and high-regret training targets using the frozen threshold h = 0.01. A SmoothL1 term and a weighted BCEWithLogits term form the joint loss. Warm-red dashed arrows indicate training supervision. True regret and its derived labels are unavailable to the evaluated group's inference pipeline. The diagram describes the audit-head computation; the inference-time ranking score is defined in Section 5.3.
