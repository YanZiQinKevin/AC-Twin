**Fig. 1 | Episode-specific factual evidence improves retrospective counterfactual effect prediction.** a Effect final-displacement error (effect-FDE) at prediction horizon 20 for eight evidence conditions. Small circles denote five independently initialized training runs; diamonds and error bars denote mean ± s.d. across runs. The dashed vertical line marks the history-only mean. b Effect-FDE as the observed same-episode factual suffix increases from 0 to 20 steps. The shaded band denotes mean ± s.d. across five training runs, and open circles show the individual runs. Performance reaches a plateau by approximately 10 steps. c Distribution of the paired episode-level difference between a matched shuffled 20-step suffix and the same-episode 20-step suffix. Positive values favour the same-episode suffix. The blue line denotes the mean difference and the blue band denotes its paired-bootstrap 95% confidence interval (10,000 resamples; n = 300 independent test episodes). The paired mean improvement is 1.13 m (95% CI, 0.86–1.39 m). Source data are provided as CSV files beside the figure.
