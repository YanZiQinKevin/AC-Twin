# Stage 4D-D2F paper figures

This directory contains copies of the final image assets and the scripts used to make them. The original experiment artifacts and confirmation results remain in `experiments/stage4_reliability/stage4d_d2f/`.

- `platform/raw_metadrive_platform.png`: genuine MetaDrive/Panda3D screenshot at the intervention time (1600 × 900).
- `platform/Fig_D2_metadrive_platform_and_branch.png` and `.pdf`: paper-ready platform panel. Its left side is the MetaDrive screenshot; its right side is a Matplotlib rendering of the ScenarioNet map and frozen trajectories. This pilot scene is selected for visual legibility, not treatment effect; the factual and counterfactual ego paths almost coincide at a red light.
- `cases/`: six separate, identically sized 600-dpi PNG panels (3600 × 1650). `success_*` is the preregistered illustrative best-of-205 eligible confirmation case; `failure_*` is the mechanically selected false-negative case. The order `01_factual`, `02_selected`, `03_oracle` is intended for user-controlled assembly. No composite is stored **inside `cases/`**; an older assembled preview remains at this directory's top level. A Chinese panel-by-panel caption and interpretation boundary is in [`cases/六张子图说明.md`](cases/六张子图说明.md).
- `scripts/`: archival copies of the Matplotlib figure scripts and their pure selection/coordinate helper. They import other AC-Twin modules and need the frozen project data and Stage 4D simulation environment; they are **not** standalone programs to run from this folder.
- `provenance/`: create-once selection and render receipts plus the six-panel manifest. These record selected scenario IDs, actions, candidate counts, source hashes, and output hashes. Image files here are byte-identical copies of the original outputs.

The qualitative case panels are not representative samples and were not used for model selection or statistical inference. In particular, “success” means the risk head flags a high-regret selection, **not** that the selected action equals the oracle action. Only the complete confirmation set supports the paper's quantitative claims. The independent MetaDrive confirmation used models retrained and frozen on that platform; these figures do not evidence zero-shot transfer of Merge weights.
