# Fig. 4 Legend

**Fig. 4 | Decision-regret auditing across three confirmation scopes.** **a–c,** Risk–coverage curves on independent sets of 2,000 ten-action groups. Blue denotes AC-Twin audit; grey denotes three standard baselines, with the frozen primary baseline darkest and heaviest. Merge ID and JOINT_OOD share frozen weights and primary baseline SEED_VOTE_ENTROPY. MetaDrive uses a platform-internally retrained pipeline and primary baseline SEED_MARGIN_STD. Each panel uses its own vertical scale. Curves include every discrete coverage prefix from 1/2,000 to 100%; the dashed line marks 80%. **d,** Relative AURC-regret and Regret@80 improvements against each scope's primary baseline, with two-sided percentile 95% intervals from 10,000 whole-group paired bootstrap resamples. Scope-specific effects are neither ranked nor pooled. Table 6 gives absolute values; Fig. 1 shows the audit workflow.
