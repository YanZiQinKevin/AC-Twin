## Negative results and historical interpretation

Earlier successful architecture reports are historical implementation evidence. The later [fair identity-continuity comparison](identity_continuity_stage/FORMAL_QA_AND_JOINT_DECISION_2026-09-06.md) was **INCONCLUSIVE** and does not establish an independent stable-ID benefit. The Merge evidence-value shape was not independently confirmed in intersection; Roundabout's measurement model was insufficient. Learned routing and initial zero-shot decision-risk transfer did not qualify for confirmation. These results are not converted into positive claims by later independently developed methods.

D2, D2R and D2E terminal outcomes remain preserved in numerical reports. D2F corrected an erroneous power treatment of scene-support proportions and completed fresh screening/confirmation; do not repeat the earlier interpretation of a structural MetaDrive reactive-rate boundary. Stage 5 stopped at `STAGE5_MERGE_COMPARISON_UNDERPOWERED`; no comparison holdout was created. Supervised controls are historical-development diagnostics, not fresh confirmation. All consumed confirmation sets remain single-use.

English Markdown files are publication copies. Two reports referenced by literal frozen hashes have byte-identical original text preserved in `release/frozen_document_originals/`; see [reproduction notes](../REPRODUCIBILITY.md). No model weights, numerical outputs or scientific code were changed in translating the documentation.
