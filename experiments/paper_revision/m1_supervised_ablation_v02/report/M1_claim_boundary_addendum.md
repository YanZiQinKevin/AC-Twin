## Joint interpretation under §8

1. C1 is supported and C2 is not established: the full information scheme adds development-level value over supervised combination of three baseline signals. The conditional contribution of the five explicit action-risk-channel families is not established; their necessity should not be emphasized.
2. C3–C5 are not established: the supervised three-scalar combination has no established development advantage over any fixed heuristic. C3 has a negative AURC estimate and positive Regret@80 estimate, neither established. Report both without selecting the favorable endpoint. This does not show that supervision explains the original heuristic-comparison advantage and does not replace the primary reference.
3. Within this development setting, the full 315-dimensional input provides additional gains over supervised use of three baseline scalars (C1). Adding regret supervision to those scalars did not reproduce Direct's performance; the incremental gain is associated with richer inputs rather than supervision alone.
